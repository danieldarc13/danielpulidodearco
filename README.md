## What you'll find on the site

- **Profile:** who I am, what I focus on, and how to reach me.
- **Featured work:** an interactive walkthrough that follows a few of my solutions step by step, from first input to business result.
- **Experience:** my career timeline, my Microsoft certifications and applied skills, my education, and my languages on the CEFR scale.
- **Solutions portfolio:** 19 case studies I can filter by category, search, and open to read the context, my contribution, how the solution works, the technologies I used and the business value.
- **Expertise and outcomes:** the capabilities I bring to delivery and the results my solutions are designed to improve.
- **Technology stack:** the platforms, services, connectors and languages I have used in delivered projects. Selecting one shows the projects where I used it.
- **Plain-language glossary:** technical terms are underlined throughout the site, and selecting one opens a short definition for non-technical readers.

The site is available in **English, French and Spanish**, and has **light and dark themes**.

## How it's built

The whole site is a single, self-contained `index.html` file:

- Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no framework and no build step
- Images (logo and portrait) embedded directly in the file
- Fonts loaded from Google Fonts
- Language and theme choices remembered in the visitor's browser
- Responsive layout for phones, tablets and desktops
- Respects the visitor's reduced-motion setting

## Hosting

The site is hosted for free with **GitHub Pages** from this repository. Every change pushed to the `main` branch is published automatically within a minute or two.

## Updating the site

1. Edit `index.html`, or replace it with a new version.
2. Commit the change to the `main` branch.
3. Wait a minute, then refresh the live site. If the old version still appears, clear your browser cache.

All content (text in the three languages, case studies, certifications and technologies) lives inside the `<script>` section of `index.html`.
